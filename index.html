FAQ
What are your opening hours?

Monday to Friday 8am to 8pm and Saturday 9am to 3pm. We don’t open on Sundays.

How long does a wash take?

Quick Steam takes about 15 minutes, Full Steam about 30 and Deep Detail about 60.

Can I wait here while my car is washed?

Yes. Sit down, order food and drinks, and we’ll call you when your car is ready.

Do I need to book?

Booking holds a bay for you, so you won’t wait. If a bay is free, walk-ins are welcome.

How can I pay?

Ask us when you arrive. We’ll confirm the payment options we accept.

Do you wash bakkies and taxis?

Yes. Call us on 071 129 9222 for a price for bigger vehicles.

Where do I send a complaint or a question?

Call or email us. Our details are at the bottom of this page.
